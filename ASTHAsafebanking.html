Stay safe from common scams

Click each alert below to learn how fraudsters operate and how to stop them.

  • Fraudsters pose as courier or law enforcement officials claiming an illegal parcel is linked to your ID.
  • Unified Finance Bank or government agencies never demand money transfers over video calls ("digital arrest").
  • Hang up immediately and verify directly through official government helplines.
  • Beware of social media groups promising guaranteed 200% returns or IPO allotments via unofficial apps.
  • Always verify that your broker or advisor is registered with SEBI before transferring funds.
  • Never download APK files shared on messaging apps for trading or banking.
  • Do not click shortened links claiming your UFB account or PAN/KYC will be blocked tonight.
  • Always open the official UFB Mobile App directly to check if any KYC update is actually pending.
